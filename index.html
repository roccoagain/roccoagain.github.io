[01] About

I'm Rocco, an electrical and computer engineering student at the University of Colorado Boulder. Competitive by nature, I enjoy engineering problems where my work gets put to the test. As a director at CU Robotics, I lead a team of software and firmware engineers, working with the other directors to bring our robots together. Check out our site! curobotics.net

I've interned as an Electrical Engineer at Thorlabs and a Backend Software Engineer at MDScripts. I'm currently working on a guitar amp that implements neural amp modeling. In my free time, I like to play guitar and build FPV drones.
